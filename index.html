Cybernetics × accounting

A firm is a feedback loop.
Its books are the sensors.

The balance sheet is the state of the system: what sits in each tank right now. The income statement and the cash flow statement are two instruments watching flows over a month. One watches the profit tank, the other watches the cash tank. They disagree for good reasons, and those reasons are where firms live or die.

You run a small company that sells DIY thermostat kits. You are the regulator: you set the levers, the market pushes back, the ledger records it, the statements report it, and you steer again.
